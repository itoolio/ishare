ishare is the definitive screen capture utility for macOS, designed with simplicity and efficiency in mind.

CAPTURE ANYTHING
• Capture a region with a precise selector, magnifier and live size readout
• Capture any window with or without its shadow, or a whole display
• Scrolling capture stitches long pages and documents into one image
• Everything runs on ScreenCaptureKit, fast and private

RECORD
• Record a display, a window or a region you draw
• Choose resolution, frame rate, HEVC, HDR, audio and microphone
• Export GIFs at the frame rate you need
• Compress large recordings automatically before uploading

ANNOTATE
• Arrows, lines, shapes, highlights, pen, marker and text
• Numbered steps, blur and pixelate to hide sensitive details
• Crop, undo and redo, then copy, save or upload in one click

UPLOAD ANYWHERE
• Imgur out of the box
• Custom uploaders for chibisafe, Zipline, copyparty, Discord webhooks and your own server
• Any S3-compatible storage: AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, DigitalOcean Spaces, Backblaze B2 and MinIO
• On Macs with Apple Intelligence, an assistant builds uploaders from a few answers

AUTOMATE
• Global keyboard shortcuts for every action
• Shortcuts actions to capture, record, upload and switch uploaders
• Share menu extension to upload files from any app

A Control Center style menu, a Liquid Glass design on macOS 26, upload history, AirDrop and Messages sharing, and translations into 11 languages round it all off.
