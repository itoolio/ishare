ishare is the definitive screen capture utility for macOS, designed with simplicity and efficiency in mind.

CAPTURE ANYTHING
• Capture a region with a precise selector, a pixel magnifier with coordinates and colors, and a measuring tool
• Capture any window with or without its shadow, or a whole display, and recapture your last region with one shortcut
• Scrolling capture stitches long pages and documents into one image
• Add a gradient background with padding, rounded corners and a shadow to window captures
• Blur chosen apps and hide the menu bar automatically for privacy
• Everything runs on ScreenCaptureKit, fast and private

RECORD
• Record a display, a window or a region you draw
• Show clicks, keystrokes and a webcam bubble in your recordings
• Capture system audio and your microphone with live level meters
• Trim recordings before saving and convert them to GIFs with size and frame rate presets
• Choose resolution, frame rate, HEVC, HDR and automatic compression before upload

ANNOTATE
• Arrows in several styles, lines, shapes, highlights, pen, marker and text
• Numbered steps, emoji and symbol stamps, spotlight, blur and pixelate
• Crop with handles and aspect presets, zoom and pan, undo and redo
• Press Done and your after-capture actions run: copy, save, share and upload

UPLOAD ANYWHERE
• Imgur out of the box
• Custom uploaders for chibisafe, Zipline, copyparty, Discord webhooks and your own server, plus ShareX .sxcu import
• Any S3-compatible storage, with optional expiring links and secrets kept in the Keychain
• Upload to several destinations at once or pick one per capture
• Upload text snippets, watch a folder for new files, and retry failed uploads automatically
• On Macs with Apple Intelligence, an assistant builds uploaders from a conversation

STAY ORGANIZED
• A compact, keyboard-friendly menu with recent uploads and previews
• Drop files on the menu bar icon or use "Upload with ishare" in Finder
• Searchable upload history with filters and server-side deletion
• Global keyboard shortcuts, Shortcuts actions and a share extension
• Back up and restore all settings and uploaders in one file

A Liquid Glass design on macOS 26, sound options, a welcome guide and translations into 11 languages round it all off.
