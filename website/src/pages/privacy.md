# Privacy policy

ishare does not collect, store or transmit personal data, and it contains no analytics or advertising.

- **Captures and recordings** stay on your Mac unless you upload them. Uploads go only to the destination you selected: Imgur, a custom uploader you configured, or your own S3-compatible storage. Those services handle your files under their own privacy policies.
- **Settings and uploaders** are stored on your Mac and, if iCloud is enabled, synced through your private iCloud key-value storage.
- **Screen recording and microphone access** are used only to create the captures and recordings you request.
- **Apple Intelligence** features run on device.
- The direct download version checks `ishare.itoolio.dev/appcast.xml` for updates. That request includes no personal data.

Questions? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/itoolio/ishare/issues).
